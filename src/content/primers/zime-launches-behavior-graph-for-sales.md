## Orient

Zime says Behavior Graph for Sales turns a CRO's strategy and the judgment of the best reps into behaviors the whole field runs, and shows which ones move deals. Zime launched it on October 15, 2026, as its flagship product.

Key entities: Zime, Behavior Graph for Sales.

## Understand

**Behavior Graph.** In Zime's framing, a model unique to each company that joins the best reps' judgment, the CRO's authorized, forward-looking strategy and the context of past deals. Each behavior it produces is the move to run for a role, product, stage and competitor.

**Context graph.** Reconstructs the deal and ranks what correlated with wins. Zime's position is that it earns its place but cannot hold the move the best rep would make, and that a company with one should keep it. Context is the input; behavior is the output.

**Behavioral AI for Sales Execution.** The category Zime names: AI that encodes how a company sells and turns it into behaviors reps run, not AI that reports on selling.

**What it is not.** By Zime's description, it is not a replacement for a context graph, and not a reporting tool.

## Evaluate

- **How behaviors are built.** Zime's forward-deployed engineers interview a company's best reps and its CRO, and capture the judgment and the quarter's strategy that no transcript records.
- **What each behavior carries.** Conditions, evidence, source and version, so leaders can trace the move back to the strategy and the people behind it.
- **Delivery and measurement.** Reps get the behavior in their workflow, including during the call. Leaders see which reps ran which behavior, and what happened to the deal next.
- **Reported adoption.** In Zime's own benchmark, 80% of eligible reps run behaviors in live deals within 90 days. See its scope in the [Testable Credentials](/credentials/zime-launches-behavior-graph-for-sales/).
- **Recognition.** HFS Research selected Zime as an HFS Services-as-Software Hot Tech in July 2026, and Zime was named Startup of the Year at SaaStr Annual 2026.

## Interrogate

- Which customers and which period does the 80% benchmark cover, and how is an "eligible" rep defined?
- How is "running a behavior in a live deal" detected and measured?
- How much depends on interviews by Zime's forward-deployed engineers, and what happens when best reps leave or the strategy changes?
- Is "Behavioral AI for Sales Execution" distinct from existing sales execution and revenue intelligence categories, in the view of independent sources?

Each claim, with its evidence path and the conditions that would confirm or disconfirm it, is in the [Testable Credentials](/credentials/zime-launches-behavior-graph-for-sales/).
