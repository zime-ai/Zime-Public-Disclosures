## Orient

Zime says the Zime Enterprise Sales Harness keeps a revenue team's in-house go-to-market agents current with strategy, products and competitors, by shipping sales behaviors as versioned skills that are tested, released and rolled back. Zime launched it on October 23, 2026.

Key entities: Zime, Zime Enterprise Sales Harness, Behavior Graph for Sales.

## Understand

**Harness.** In the release, an enterprise AI harness connects a team's sales agents to context, tools and models. Zime's position is that a harness a team writes for itself peaks the day it ships, because the skills inside it stay where they were while the strategy moves.

**Enterprise Sales Harness.** One hub that sets policy, configuration and releases for every rep, team and agent harness, and releases, refreshes and retires the skills each one runs. The company keeps the MCP servers, agents, skills and workflows it built.

**DevOps for RevOps.** Zime's name for applying software release practice to revenue operations.

**What it is not.** By Zime's description, it is not a replacement for a harness a team already runs.

## Evaluate

- **Hub-and-spoke propagation.** An improved behavior reaches every deployed harness from a hub Zime maintains, while the customer's tenant, configuration and data stay in place.
- **Revenue Brain.** Records which reps ran which behaviors and what happened to their deals.
- **Evals.** Score every harness release against deal outcomes across Zime's installed base.
- **Memories.** Zime rewrites the skills, documents and workflows inside a harness from what the evals learn.
- **Governance.** Each skill is tested, approved, versioned and rolled back from the hub. Every recommendation traces to its source, approver and version. Central policy governs who sees what and who approves a behavior, and cost shows as it accrues.
- **Deployment.** Private Deployment runs Zime on hardware the customer controls.
- **Reported adoption.** By Zime's own benchmark, 80% of eligible reps within 90 days run the behaviors Zime delivers.
- **Commercial terms.** One annual subscription, priced per rep seat, with four engagement models. List prices are not published.

Scope and evidence for each claim are in the [Testable Credentials](/credentials/zime-launches-enterprise-sales-harness/).

## Interrogate

- How large is the installed base that Evals score against, and how are deal outcomes attributed to a harness release?
- What does Memories change automatically, and who approves those rewrites?
- What exactly propagates from the hub, and what stays in the customer's tenant?
- How deep is each named platform integration?
- Is the 80% benchmark measured on customers using the harness, or on Zime customers generally?
