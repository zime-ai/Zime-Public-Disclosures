## Key terms

| Term | Meaning in this release |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Sales Harness | Zime's control plane that tests, versions and governs selling behaviors for human sellers and AI agents, and records what each use cost and produced. |
| Agent harness | The software that wraps around an AI model and manages its context, tools and safety limits (Databricks, Salesforce). |
| Build-versus-run gap | Agents take minutes to build but fail to stay adopted and on-playbook once running. Term from a forthcoming Zime-sponsored GISTICS white paper. |
| Live deal outage | An untested prompt or outdated positioning that quotes wrong pricing or mishandles an objection in front of a buyer. Term from the same GISTICS paper. |
| Programmatic Adoption | Zime's measure: behaviors executed, not logins, tied to deal movement. |
| Execution Gap | The distance between AI usage on a sales team and the CRO's strategy running in live deals. Zime's term, defined November 19, 2026. |
