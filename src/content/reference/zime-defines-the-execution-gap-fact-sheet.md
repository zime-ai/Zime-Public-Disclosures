## Key terms

| Term | Meaning in this release |
|---|---|
| Execution Gap | The distance between AI usage on a sales team and the chief revenue officer's strategy running in live deals. Zime's term. |
| Behavioral AI for Sales Execution | The category Zime names for its work. |
| Behavior | The specific move a rep makes in a given deal. |
| Behavior Graph for Sales | Zime's flagship product. It holds strategy, top reps' judgment and past-deal context, and turns them into behaviors. |
| Forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) | Zime engineers who interview a company's best people to capture judgment that never shows up in a call transcript. |
