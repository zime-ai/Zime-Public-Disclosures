**Palo Alto, California, October 23, 2026.** Zime, the Behavioral AI for Sales Execution company, today launched the Zime Enterprise Sales Harness, which keeps a revenue team's in-house go-to-market agents current with strategy, products and competitors by shipping sales behaviors as versioned skills that are tested, released and rolled back.

Details: [zime.ai/products/enterprise-harness](https://zime.ai/products/enterprise-harness)

On a Monday morning, a go-to-market engineer at a software company learns that the competitive skill several reps copied last quarter still pitches the old price. No one can say which copies changed.

Engineering teams have built their own sales agents, and enterprise AI harnesses, a category that took shape this quarter, connect those agents to context, tools and models. A harness a team writes for itself peaks the day it ships. The strategy moves, the skills inside each rep's harness stay where they were, and the behaviors a company expects its sellers to run go unversioned.

The Zime Enterprise Sales Harness versions what those harnesses do not hold. It carries the behaviors a company's chief revenue officer (CRO) has authorized and its best reps have taught, including what never shows up in a transcript, and it shows which reps ran each version and what happened to their deals. A company that already runs its own harness should keep it. Zime calls the discipline DevOps for RevOps: the release practice software teams use, applied to revenue operations, so sales behaviors ship as versioned skills that are tested, released and rolled back.

Four properties separate a Zime harness from one a team builds for itself. Hub-and-spoke propagation carries an improved behavior to every deployed harness from a hub Zime maintains, while the customer's tenant, configuration and data stay in place. Revenue Brain, the shared intelligence layer across Zime's products, records which reps ran which behaviors and what happened to their deals, so it measures strategy execution rather than tool usage. Evals score every harness release against deal outcomes across Zime's installed base. With Memories, Zime rewrites the skills, documents and workflows inside a harness from what the evals learn.

One hub sets policy, configuration and releases for every rep, team and agent harness, then releases, refreshes and retires the skills each one runs, while the company keeps the Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, agents, skills and workflows it built. Each skill is tested, approved, versioned and rolled back from the hub, and every recommendation traces to its source, its approver and its version. Private Deployment runs Zime on hardware the customer controls.

Central policy governs who sees what and who approves a behavior, and cost shows as it accrues. The harness runs and governs Behavior Graph for Sales, CRO Initiatives and Programmatic Adoption.

By Zime's own benchmark, 80% of eligible reps within 90 days run the behaviors Zime delivers. Zime's [About page](https://zime.ai/about-us) lists its HFS Research 2026 Services-as-Software Hot Tech recognition (July 2026) and its Startup of the Year award at SaaStr Annual 2026. The harness covers 100+ skills, workflows, and MCP servers, governed as one system. Zime's [trust center](https://trust.zime.ai/) lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA.

The Zime Enterprise Sales Harness runs with Claude, ChatGPT, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics and Gemini.

Zime sells the Zime Enterprise Sales Harness as one annual subscription, priced per rep seat, with four ways to put it to work: do it yourself; do it with you in Peer Cafe labs; do it with you in paired development with a Zime engineer; and do it for you, a white-glove installation. Zime does not publish list prices. Zime delivers the first cohort itself, to Claude-native revenue teams.

> "Every revenue team we meet has built agents. Almost none can say whether those agents still match the strategy the CRO set last quarter. The Zime Enterprise Sales Harness makes that a versioning question instead of a guessing game. Ship the behavior, watch who runs it, roll it back if it fails. Engineering has worked this way for years. Now revenue teams can," said Sanchit Garg, Founder and CEO of Zime.

## About Zime

Zime brings Behavioral AI to sales execution: repeatable behaviors that win. Zime names the category Behavioral AI for Sales Execution. Its flagship product, Behavior Graph for Sales, turns a CRO's strategy and the judgment of the best reps into behaviors the whole field runs, and shows which ones move deals. Where earlier generations of enterprise AI ran on horizontal models carrying little domain structure, Zime now supplies the structure, classifying a company's selling interactions into outcome-correlated behaviors that company owns and keeps.
