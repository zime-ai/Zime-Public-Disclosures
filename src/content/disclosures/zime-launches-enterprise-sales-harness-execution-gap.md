**PALO ALTO, Calif., November 26, 2026.** Zime, the Behavioral AI for Sales Execution company, today launched Zime Enterprise Sales Harness, a control plane that tests, versions and governs selling behaviors for every human seller and artificial intelligence (AI) agent on an enterprise revenue team, and records what each use cost and produced.

Details: [zime.ai/products/enterprise-harness](https://zime.ai/products/enterprise-harness)

Google Cloud's [2025 DORA research](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2025_state_of_ai_assisted_software_development.pdf), drawn from nearly 5,000 technology professionals, found that AI acts as an amplifier: it raises software delivery throughput but still increases delivery instability, and its gains depend on strong internal platforms. A forthcoming Zime-sponsored GISTICS white paper finds the same build-versus-run gap in sales: revenue teams spin up prompts and customer relationship management (CRM) agents in minutes, then watch reps ignore them or agents go off-playbook on live calls.

When a chief revenue officer (CRO) changes competitive strategy on Monday, one seller's agent gets the update, another's does not, and by Friday two buyers hear two different answers. The GISTICS paper calls this a live deal outage: an untested prompt or outdated positioning that quotes wrong pricing or mishandles an objection in front of a buyer. "A crashed service can roll back. A misquoted price cannot," the paper states.

Disclosure: Zime sponsored the GISTICS paper, and its author, Michael Jay Moon, serves as Zime's chief marketing officer.

[Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/ai-harness) and [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/ai-agents/agent-harness/) both describe an agent as a model plus a harness: the software that wraps around the model and manages its context, tools and safety limits. Zime recommends that a company keep the enterprise-wide harness that registers its agents, connects tools and controls access.

Zime Enterprise Sales Harness governs the selling behaviors the CRO authorized, not only the agents that carry them. A company updates its strategy once in Behavior Graph for Sales; the harness stress-tests each change with evaluations on the company's own deals and competitors before release, then distributes it to every rep and agent. Each behavior carries the strategy that authorized it, its approver and its version. Teams approve, version and roll back skills from one hub, and the company keeps what it built.

Central policy sets who sees what and who approves a behavior, and each prompt passes a policy and security check before it runs. Guardrail settings let an agent suggest, act alone, escalate or request human approval as policy permits, so it can log CRM updates on its own while non-standard pricing and contract terms go to a person. Model routing considers the task, quality needs, sensitivity and cost, and the hub shows spend as it accrues by team, workflow and agent.

Zime counts the result as Programmatic Adoption: behaviors executed, not logins, tied to deal movement. Zime reports that at Versa Networks, the share of solution engineers running expected technical discovery rose from 20% to 79% in three months, alongside a 12% rise in technical evaluations won. Zime's figures do not come from a controlled study.

Zime reports more than 100 skills, workflows and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers governed as one system. The harness runs on Claude and ChatGPT assistants, Slack and Microsoft Teams collaboration software, and Salesforce, HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics CRM platforms. Revenue teams can book a demo at [zime.ai/demo](https://zime.ai/demo).

> "Revenue teams have built agents, and the hard question is whether those agents still run the strategy the CRO set this quarter. The Enterprise Sales Harness turns that into a release question, the way engineering treats software: test the behavior before a buyer hears it, see who ran it, and roll it back if it fails," said Sanchit Garg, Founder and CEO of Zime.

## About Zime

Zime brings Behavioral AI to sales execution: repeatable behaviors that win. Zime names the category Behavioral AI for Sales Execution. Its flagship product, Behavior Graph for Sales, turns a CRO's strategy and the judgment of the best reps into behaviors the whole field runs, and shows which ones move deals. Where earlier generations of enterprise AI ran on horizontal models carrying little domain structure, Zime now supplies the structure, classifying a company's selling interactions into outcome-correlated behaviors that company owns and keeps.
