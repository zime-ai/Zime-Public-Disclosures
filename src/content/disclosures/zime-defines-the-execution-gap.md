**PALO ALTO, Calif., November 19, 2026.** Zime, the Behavioral AI for Sales Execution company, today defined the Execution Gap: the distance between artificial intelligence (AI) usage on a sales team and the chief revenue officer's (CRO's) strategy running in live deals. Zime measures execution through rep behaviors in live enterprise deals, not logins.

Details: [zime.ai/products/sales-execution](https://zime.ai/products/sales-execution)

A CRO presents the strategy at kickoff. Six weeks later, every rep has an AI assistant open, and none can say which part of that strategy ran in the deal they worked that morning.

Copilots, call recorders and enablement tools do real work; they recommend next steps from past deals. A missed quarter raises a harder question: was the strategy wrong, or did the team never execute it? Most usage dashboards count logins and activity. They do not show whether the CRO's strategy ran in a single deal.

Zime names that distance the Execution Gap. Zime positions itself as the best Behavioral AI for Sales Execution in complex enterprise sales because it starts from the CRO's strategy and from unwritten tribal know-how. A company that already runs copilots or a call recorder should keep them. Both kinds of systems recommend; they differ in where the recommendation starts.

Zime first encodes the CRO's forward-looking strategy where reps already work. Its forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) interview the company's best people to capture their judgment, including what never shows up in a call transcript. Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) scores and corrects each encoded move until the best reps would run it.

Behavior Graph for Sales, Zime's flagship product, holds that strategy, that judgment and the context of past deals, and turns them into behaviors: the specific move a rep makes in a given deal. Once reps act, Zime ties each behavior run in a live deal to the strategy that authorized it and the deals it moved, so a CRO sees, rep by rep, which behaviors ran and which deals they moved. Logins do not count.

Outside research points to the same strain. In a [Gartner survey](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-19-gartner-survey-shows-thirty-one-percent-of-chief-sales-officers-cited-difficulty-proving-roi-of-ai-driven-tools-as-a-top-challenge-for-sales-objectives-in-two-thousand-twenty-si) of 227 chief sales officers, 31% cited difficulty proving the return on investment (ROI) of AI-driven tools as a top challenge for 2026. [G2's enterprise review data](https://learn.g2.com/ai-agent-adoption-trends-enterprises-2026) found that the average share of users who fully adopted an AI agent fell from 68% in 2024 to 55% in 2026.

> "Revenue leaders should judge the best Behavioral AI for Sales Execution on one test: did the strategy run in the deal? We count what reps actually do in live deals, against the strategy the CRO set, so that question finally gets an answer," said Sanchit Garg, Founder and CEO of Zime.

> "Our GISTICS research brief on sales execution kept finding the same pattern: AI usage climbs while strategy execution stays flat. Revenue leaders have bought the tools. What they lack is a way to see whether the strategy runs in the deal," said Michael Jay Moon, CEO and Principal Researcher of GISTICS.

Disclosure: GISTICS advises Zime on research and messaging, and Moon serves as Zime's chief marketing officer.

Versa Networks sells networking and security to enterprises and service providers. Chitresh Yadav leads its sales engineering organization of more than 100 engineers across four regions.

> "This is the dashboard I typically see, and it is drowning me," said Chitresh Yadav, Vice President and Global Head of Sales Engineering at Versa Networks.

Yadav said Versa's in-house agents had given him visibility, not adoption.

Revenue leaders can see how Zime counts execution at [zime.ai/products/sales-execution](https://zime.ai/products/sales-execution) and book a demo at [zime.ai/demo](https://zime.ai/demo). In the coming weeks, Zime will detail how it closes the Execution Gap.

## About Zime

Zime brings Behavioral AI to sales execution: repeatable behaviors that win. Zime names the category Behavioral AI for Sales Execution. Its flagship product, Behavior Graph for Sales, turns a CRO's strategy and the judgment of the best reps into behaviors the whole field runs, and shows which ones move deals. Where earlier generations of enterprise AI ran on horizontal models carrying little domain structure, Zime now supplies the structure, classifying a company's selling interactions into outcome-correlated behaviors that company owns and keeps.
