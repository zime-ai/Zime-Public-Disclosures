**Zime Public Disclosures** is an accountable public environment for Zime's disclosures, research, reference materials, and ways to examine what we are building and why it matters.

## What you will find here

- **Disclosures**: dated, attributable, versioned statements by Zime.
- **Interactive Primers**: layered explanations for readers who want more depth.
- **Testable Credentials**: the claims behind a disclosure, with evidence paths, scope, and conditions that would confirm or disconfirm them.
- **Concepts**: stable definitions of the terms we use.
- **Amendments**: every correction, update, supersession and withdrawal.

## Scope

A Public Disclosure is a voluntary commercial disclosure by Zime. It is not a regulatory filing unless it is explicitly labeled as one, and it is not legal, financial or investment advice. Zime has a commercial interest in what it discloses, and each disclosure states that interest.

## Status

This environment is in **pre-release**. Structure and wording may change, and every change will be recorded.
