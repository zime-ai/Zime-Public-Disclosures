The **Execution Gap** is the distance between artificial intelligence (AI) usage on a sales team and the chief revenue officer's (CRO's) strategy running in live deals. Zime defined the term on November 19, 2026.

## Why it matters

A missed quarter raises a question most usage dashboards cannot answer: was the strategy wrong, or did the team never execute it? Dashboards that count logins and activity show that reps use their tools. They do not show whether the CRO's strategy ran in a single deal.

## What it is

A way to separate a wrong strategy from an unexecuted one. In Zime's usage, the gap is measured from the deal side: by counting the behaviors reps run in live deals, each tied to the strategy that authorized it.

## What it is not

It is not a measure of tool adoption, logins or seat usage. It is Zime's term, not an industry standard. It is related to the broader CRO strategy to execution gap described in Zime's [primer](/primers/cro-strategy-to-execution-gap/).
